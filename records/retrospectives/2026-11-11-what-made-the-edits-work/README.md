# What made the past edits work: a read-only study and the configuration it proposes

November 11, 2026 (CT), by the session weight-research-perfomance, at Marcos's request to study the theory and the successful weight edits done in the past,
including the one that generalized and the first training run that also moved almost all errors to correct, to understand why they worked, what they
share and how they were calculated, and to come up with a set of parameters and a training configuration that would succeed, or at least a proposal.

It is a reading of records. No model, card or test was run, nothing in a run folder was written, the sealed set was not opened, and nothing in it
is a round-two result. It decides nothing: the objective and the acceptance of the controlled training are Astra's, its solver and sweep are
weight-research-fable's, and the method's steps are Marcos's. It is a proposal to them and not guidance for a run.

- `PROPOSAL.md`: the answer. Which records the two runs he named can be, why the edits that repaired did, what broke the model each time, how
  each edit was calculated, the proposed configuration with each parameter's source, what to measure first, the decisions that are Marcos's with
  what each option causes, and what is not known. Section 14 is what the study's last reader changed; section 15 is what this session checked
  against the records afterwards and one correction it made; section 16 is what Astra's reading of the proposal corrected, each point fixed in
  place.
- `ask_in_overlay_units.py`, `.out`: the check behind that correction.
- `notes/`: the working notes of the study's seats: ten readers (one body of records each), four analysts, the proposers and their skeptics,
  the answer before its last reading, and the note from weight-research-astra that the later phases were given
  (`colleague-note-given-to-the-later-phases.txt`).
- `result.json`: everything the study returned, as returned (it does not carry the corrections of sections 15 and 16): the answer as data, the last reader's critique with the sixteen records it opened, the three
  proposals with their nine skeptics' verdicts, the four analyses and the ten readers' maps.
- `workflow.js`: the study as it ran: ten readers, four analysts, three proposals each attacked by three skeptics, one seat that decides, one
  last reader with no stake, one revision. 29 seats, about 50 minutes, from 12:14 to 1:05 AM CT. The paths in it and in `result.json` are the session's scratch folder; the
  notes they name are the ones under `notes/`.
