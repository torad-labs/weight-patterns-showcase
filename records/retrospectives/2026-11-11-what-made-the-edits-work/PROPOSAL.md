# What made the past edits work, and the configuration that follows

November 11, 2026 (CT). Written by the deciding seat of the what-worked study. Read only: no model, card or test was run. READ = I or a skeptic opened the record at the cited line or key; INFER = arithmetic or reasoning on records; MAP = taken from a reader's notes and not reopened by me.

## In short

All the edits that repaired the calculation did four things. They fitted individual rows of real answers at the edited matrix's actual inputs. They aimed at a correct-versus-wrong difference at that matrix's own output or state. They used a regularised solve. They wrote the result once in BF16. Collapse was avoided when the fit held correct rows inside it or followed path order. Correct answers were kept only where protection was part of the fit itself (old A2's success rows; C, D and E4's exact constraints). No edit on record repaired the calculation and also kept every correct answer.

The training already declared, controlled-bf16-1 as revised at 00:38 CT today, holds every one of those ingredients and avoids every cause of collapse on record. All three proposals ended up close to it, and the parts of them that differed mostly did not survive their skeptics. So the proposal is controlled-bf16-1 as revised, with readings added beside its verdicts, four card measurements, and four decisions for you, each with a recommended option. The calibration gap sits in four places: the row bound, which single BF16 matrices already leave on a few unused-answer cells; how far the ask goes; what text later sweeps fit on; and the solver. The solver is not ruled out. Raising its cap to 160 iterations gains 0.025 at layer 3's attention, about 125 times the 0.0002 a repeat moves, and nothing at layer 15's feed-forward, the widest kind of matrix. Where the bounds bind, the solve is cut short with 0.22 of its slope left, and why it is slow there "was looked for and not found" (READ records/development/2026-11-10-controlled-training/README.md line 24). You said "then the problem is thje solver" (journal/2026-11-10-controlled-training.md:62).

### 2a. D1, which you called the best result

What D1 did (READ D-background-sensitivity/run/REPORT.md:46 onward): one matrix (layer 16 attention output), the same donor output difference and design input as A, read through q = (M + λI)^-1 k with M the second moment of background requests, normalised so q^T k = 1, λ = 0.05 trace(M)/2048 (actual 0.00921740181182784). "No controls, held-out background requests or other physics contexts fit this read key. There was one candidate, with no ridge, amplitude or layer sweep." Outcome: chains 7→9 of 28, verdicts 16→17, all 12 controls retained, fresh physics 1/4→1/4 (REPORT.md:74-77).

Why it repaired while breaking none of 7: by its own record, size was counted by action on inputs outside the task. "D1's realized matrix change is 0.0452194% of the original matrix norm, 1.5248 times A1's change, while its held-out-background RMS projection action is 0.14497 times A1's ... **Matrix norm alone is therefore not a transferable measure of useful strength or interference.**" The same page says the 5% ridge "was designed, not discovered as an optimum" (READ READINESS.md:44). In the three fields: λ's measured_from is the background moment (yes), its 0.05 factor has no derivation, and nothing gives it scales_with.

Is that ingredient carried by controlled-bf16-1? INFER, from its declaration and tube.py as read by two skeptics: no. Its ridge counts change from the original matrix (identity, from ridge_unit), and its bounds cover only the 60 physics answers' rows and their prompts. No ordinary request enters the solve or the verdicts. d2-path tried a version of D1's measure (the ridge in the ordinary-input measure, 8 requests). It removed the fourth-token shift and still broke 23/30 (READ ROUND-2.md:9). But d2 also had no correct rows, so the record does not isolate the measure. What D1's ingredient protects is ordinary text. controlled-bf16-1 does not read ordinary text, so whether the training keeps it is unknown. Section 5 adds D1's background action as a reading beside the verdicts. It is not a bound.

### 2b. The theory against the record

The theory (READ hypothesis/RESIDUAL-TRACE-THEORY.md, RESIDUAL-TRACE-HANDOFF.md, TWO-RESEARCH-FRONTS.md, with the theory reader's notes at notes/read-theory/notes.md) makes these claims. A capability leaves recurring portions of a trace. Where correct and wrong answers part, the overlaps show a gap. Closing it repairs the wrong answers, and "Because the gap belongs to the capability and not to one question, the fix should carry over" (READ ROUND-2.md:18). Edit location, number of matrices and form "are outcomes of trace comparison, not fixed historical parameters" (READ TWO-RESEARCH-FRONTS.md:21). A construction "must explain which observed part determined it" (READ :15). An edit must be realised in the deployed format (THEORY:338-342, via the reader).

What the record supports:
- **Repair of the targeted calculation by closing a gap: supported.** Every rung aimed at the state gap repaired the targeted operation in 27-30 of 30 (READ ROUND-2.md:184 wording; the measures differ by rung, see section 3).
- **"Leave the matching parts alone": supported, and stronger than the theory states it.** Removing old A2's success rows took it from 25/36 and 7/31 to 0/36 and 31/31, and "Preservation changed the fitted direction" (READ STUDY.md:113; READINESS.md:65). On Nov 10 you put protection inside the training (journal line 17).
- **Location as an outcome: supported by its failures.** Every round-two rung's 18 matrices came from a solver stall, and the groups' inputs separate most at layers 14-23, where no rung reached (input-overlap, READ by two skeptics).
- **Native format: supported.** Every physics repair was written once in BF16, and you closed float32 (ROUND-2.md:7).

What the record does not show:
- **That recurrence is what carries over.** The edit you named as generalizing had a pairwise target, "not a recurring difference" (READ STUDY.md:15). a2-path, the one recurring, state-target, path-ordered edit, carried 7/21 to round one's questions, shown by text only.
- **Any mechanism of transfer.** No captured traces exist on any question outside a fit (READ MECHANISM-STUDY.md:60).

Two requirements the theory does not state, which the later failures exposed (INFER from b2 and c2):
- Protection has to hold on the edited model's own text, not only on the recorded text. b2: 51/60 leave the given text at the fourth token.
- An edit cannot protect rows whose inputs equal the rows it must move. c2: at the opening of layers 0-8 the damaged and repaired inputs lie 0.0-0.8% outside each other's span.

On status, quoted exactly: the Nov 6 handoff says "This is a motivated hypothesis, not a completed local discovery or a guaranteed safe editing method" (READ RESIDUAL-TRACE-HANDOFF.md:72). The theory adds: "The fit does not by itself demonstrate a shared extraction method ... or guarantee that every capability will yield a recognizable trace" (READ THEORY:315-318). Your Nov 10 journal says "The hypothesis is unchanged" (READ journal/2026-11-10-controlled-training.md:46). Your evening framing, that the method has been shown several times to work, including generalization, comes from the task text and is not in a file I read. The records support "proven" for the targeted calculation inside the fit. Generalisation is shown on text only: old A2's 9/31 on a seen set, a2-path's 7/21, A's sled.

On your RL analogy: the theory itself uses reinforcement as its picture, saying reinforcement "makes the coordinated transformations supporting such procedures more reliably expressed" (THEORY:213-219, via the reader). In those terms the record has measured the direction (the gap where it exceeds 2 chance) and the per-cell trust region (V/30, 2V/15). The step (the ask distance), the rows of later passes (on- or off-policy) and the inner solve have not been measured. Decisions 2 and 3 are where those enter.

- **c2-path:** at the opening of layers 0-8 the damaged and repaired rows are the same inputs (0.0-0.8% outside each other's span at the median, at most 4.4%; READ c2-protected/result.json via skeptic). "The fit served neither ask" (READ ROUND-2.md:9).
- **d2-path:** the target came from one error subtype with no correct rows. It taught counting the descent on both sides (31/60). The status line says "their traces lie back at the wrong pattern at places 312 to 335" (READ ROUND-2.md:9). The run's own entry corrects that. It was "an exploratory reading after the result: not declared, not a test, not a rule for where to edit ... So at 312-335 an undeclared reading saw a difference of projection, outside the 142-313 window the rungs were judged on. That is reason to extend the readings to place 479. It does not show a return to the wrong pattern.

The common thread: no damage-free multi-matrix edit exists on record. The breaks come from protection that was missing, off the model's own text, or placed where the inputs cannot be told apart. They also come from any dose set after the fact, and from a refit that does not count movement from the original.
