- **Positions and operation inventory:** the active full trace has every captured position and all 105 merges. The analyst's current prospective plan explicitly examines 310–312, the three positions before reference 313, and every merge there. It also examines earlier heading and quantity positions. Existing full computational DTW paths supply mappings; no matched output token is used to create new correspondence. - **Numerical feature:** the directional part of the old `through` residual is exactly a turn difference, `T_query - sum_j alpha_j T_success,j`, where `T = unit(output) - unit(input)`. Current `turn_pair.turns` retains that ingredient. The old diagnostic's log-size coordinate, incoming-context mixture, case-conditioned direction and separate own-addition/state views are not the same measurement as the current one-reference turn. Therefore it would be wrong to say all old evidence was absent from the turn representation, and equally wrong to assert the complete old diagnostic is reproduced by storing turns.
