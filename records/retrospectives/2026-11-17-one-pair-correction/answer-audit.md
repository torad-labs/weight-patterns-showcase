# The same physics can produce different computational traces

Marcos pointed out on November 17 that a correct-prompt trace could diverge when the edited model expresses the same solution differently. The distinction matters in the actual captures. The numerical checks below use the original development questions, the stated parameters and the model's generated calculations. They are outcome diagnostics outside training. The learner still receives only its declared computational loss.

| Prompt at frozen 348 | Physics from the original question | Original model's response | Edited model's response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Selected correct, `expanded-004-h1` | Available 16 + 40 − 12 = **44 J**. Required **20 J**. **YES**. | Gets the descent sign, dissipation, climb, and verdict right | Gets the same steps, energies and verdict right; rewords the explanation |
| Fitted, `discovery-05-channel-h1` | Available 64 + 40 − 13 = **91 J**. Required **20 J**. **YES**. | Gives **11 J, 20 J, NO** | Gives the correct intermediate calculation and **91 J, 20 J, YES** |
| Unfitted development, `discovery-10-channel-h1` | Available 96 + 30 − 11 = **115 J**. Required **30 J**. **YES**. | Subtracts the descent, reports **55 J**, double-counts height in the climb (**60 J**), concludes **NO** | Still subtracts the descent and reports **55 J**. Corrects the climb to **30 J** and concludes **YES** |

Both comparison prompts move farther from the fixed reference trace at 348. The selected correct solution remains correct and the development solution partially improves. A single-reference trace loss and the validity of a solution are distinct observations. The examples do not establish that the internal algorithm is identical or that the model's capability improved across a population. ### A preference flip at an identical prefix

The original fitted-prompt generation has the exact token sequence bound to the original loss measurement. [Original fitted response](../../../studies/04-round-2-overlap/runs/sent-overlap/capture/batch-discovery-05-channel-h1/discovery-05-channel-h1/result.json) · [Fitted response at 348](../../../studies/04-round-2-overlap/runs/vector-chance-final-experiment-installed-348-recovery/loop/0/capture/raw/discovery-05-channel-h1/result.json).

On the correct prompt the original and edited models generated the first 266 tokens identically. At zero-based generated index 266 both had just written “The required final”. The original preferred “ energy” to “ potential” by 0.419669 logits. The edited model preferred “ potential” to “ energy” by 0.202913. Their continuations were “energy” and “potential energy”, with the same 20 J result. The model used greedy decoding. At their respective verdict positions the YES-minus-NO margins were **12.34** and **17.26** logits. Those final prefixes differ, so the change in final margin cannot be attributed entirely to the weights independently of history. Top-eight logits plus a digest were retained; exact full-vocabulary softmax probabilities cannot be reconstructed from those data. ### A temporary failure and recovery on the fitted prompt

In the earlier crossed-history experiment the fitted prompt already had correct arithmetic at both endpoints. Both models calculated initial kinetic energy 64 J, descent gain 40 J, post-friction energy 91 J, required energy 20 J, and YES. Fresh trace loss nevertheless fell **56.26% between 175 and 189** on the matched device. On this case, that additional drop bought closer agreement with the chosen trace; the recorded calculations and verdict already earned full credit at 175. [Step 175 free generation](../../../studies/04-round-2-overlap/runs/vector-chance-crossed-history-175-189/weights-175-free/raw/discovery-05-channel-h1/result.json) · [Step 189 free generation](../../../studies/04-round-2-overlap/runs/vector-chance-crossed-history-175-189/weights-189-free/raw/discovery-05-channel-h1/result.json).

The first token difference was in the final comparison, after both energies had already been calculated. Step 175 labeled the available energy with its variable name, while 189 expanded the label to “Energy at the start of the climb”. Each then restated 91 J available and 20 J required. The [four-way native comparison](../../development/2026-11-17-crossed-history/README.md) found that supplying the later history at either fixed weight state accounted for 99.66–99.80% of the diagonal loss decrease, depending on the order of interventions. For this interval the measured gain mainly rewarded how an already solved problem was restated. That interpretation cannot be carried over to the episodes where the arithmetic changed.

Across all 46 distinct fresh observations from 317 through 362, the model produced the correct three requested outputs at 41 checkpoints. At 321–323, 345 and 356 it instead reported **11 J, 20 J, NO**, consistent with subtracting the descent energy. It returned to **91 J, 20 J, YES** at 324, 346 and 357. This verifies three temporary failures and recoveries of the measured fitted behavior in this interval. The step-355, 356 and 357 trace losses were 0.00006392913, 0.00073684654 and 0.00006457637. The latter two outcomes demonstrate that a transient spike can coincide with a real, reversible reasoning error. They do not establish a model-wide collapse. [355](../../../studies/04-round-2-overlap/runs/vector-chance-final-experiment-installed-348-recovery/loop/7/capture/raw/discovery-05-channel-h1/result.json) · [356](../../../studies/04-round-2-overlap/runs/vector-chance-final-experiment-installed-348-recovery/loop/8/capture/raw/discovery-05-channel-h1/result.json) · [357](../../../studies/04-round-2-overlap/runs/vector-chance-final-experiment-installed-348-recovery/loop/9/capture/raw/discovery-05-channel-h1/result.json).

The first differing choice in the 355–357 episode came immediately after the identical prefix “Here is the”. The logit margin of “ compact” over “ calculation” went from **+0.04019** at 355 to **−0.00755** at 356 to **+0.02144** at 357. The complete generated token sequences at 355 and 357 were identical. At 356 the model subsequently subtracted 40 J on the descent. The early near tie identifies where the paths first separated; this comparison alone cannot tell whether forcing that one word would cause the later sign error.
