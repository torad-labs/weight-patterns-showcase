The model currently spends much of its fitted loss on a single computation position on its 612-position route. At 355, current position 374 is aligned with reference positions 373 and 374. Across the 105 merges this contributes 0.00003471261 out of 0.00006392913. Positive tolerance already allows some movement. At 37 merges the two allowed balls around their respective reference turns are disjoint.

For each merge, let the reference turns have distance (d), tolerance radii (r_a,r_b), and recorded pair masses (a,b). Assigning one arbitrary turn to the shared current position costs at least

    (a b / (a + b)) max(d − r_a − r_b, 0)².

This value is attained in the relaxed problem by placing the turn on the line segment between the two targets. All other current positions have a single target and can be matched exactly in this relaxation. The masses already include the merge normalization. Summing gives **0.00000288720**. Even a perfectly optimized network with this *same alignment and tolerance* cannot exceed **99.0124%** reduction. A new path can have a different bound. At historical 203 every current position mapped to a single reference position, making this particular bound zero while measured loss remained positive. ## Preservation, transfer and the decision

The training target remains the fixed original reference trace. No step of the learning loop regenerates that reference. The following two comparisons used read-only models on a second GPU, with full fresh computational measurements and no gradient. Both used original-model controls on that device.

| Separately evaluated prompt | Original native loss | Frozen 348 native loss | Outcome |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Original reference prompt | 0.00000013003 | 0.00009352030 | Increased at all 105 merges |
| Next originally wrong development prompt in manifest order | 0.00032040365 | 0.00052137736 | Increased 62.72%; 90 merges rose and 15 fell |

The frozen reference cannot reveal what a later model will compute if given that reference prompt. The first comparison supplies that observation. The second prompt was chosen in advance, saw no update, and belongs to the known development cohort. On the selected correct prompt the model retained the same correct intermediate arithmetic and verdict. On the unfitted prompt the edited response corrected the required energy and verdict while retaining a wrong sign on the descent. An earlier assessment used these higher losses to infer failed preservation and negative transfer of the behavior. The recorded solutions contradict that inference. [Arithmetic and decoding audit](answer-audit.md) · [Reference trace comparison](../../development/2026-11-17-reference-preservation/reference-preservation-result.md) · [Prospective selection](development-case.md) · [Development trace comparison](../../development/2026-11-17-reference-preservation/development-transfer-result.md).

The four additional complete native generations show why a percentage alone is a poor guide to marginal value on this pair. At 175, 189 and 348 the fitted problem and reference problem both have correct energies and verdicts. The unfitted development problem at all three checkpoints has the same partial improvement, with correct required energy and verdict but the wrong descent sign. Fitted original-baseline reduction at those three snapshots was **66.70%, 85.43% and 78.04%** respectively. The unfitted prompt's fixed-reference trace loss was lower than its original 0.00032040 at 175 and 189 (0.00016123 and 0.00016113) and higher at 348 (0.00052138). Its arithmetic had the same error in all three. These observations do not locate a universal sufficient percentage or show continuous preservation between the sampled checkpoints.

**Decision.** Treat 95% as a possible reporting milestone. There is no measured reason to demand 95%, 97% or 98% before evaluating utility. For the one-pair experiment, report the fresh decrease, local errors, and stability across subsequent weight updates. For selecting a repair procedure, look for the earliest evaluated setting that sustains the correct reasoning on the fitted prompt, preserves a previously correct solution, and repairs the intermediate error on an unfitted development case. Keep the trace scores alongside independent checks of the required operations and quantities, allowing valid alternative solutions. On this model an increase in single-reference trace loss can accompany either a change of wording or an actual reasoning error. The original control distributions overlap and cannot validate a universal cutoff. [Calibration on all 30 original development traces](../2026-11-17-one-pair-stopping-calibration/README.md).
