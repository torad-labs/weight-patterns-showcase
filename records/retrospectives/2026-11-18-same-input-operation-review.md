# Independent review of same-input replay

## What the comparison measures

An upstream descent repair on the fitted question raises energy at the entrance to friction from 24 J to 104 J. Both answers then subtract 13 J correctly. A successful repair therefore moves both sides of the friction calculation by 80 J. This gives the replay an observed preservation outcome against which to judge an internal score. The experiment uses existing developments on one question. The two successful interventions start from an early trained checkpoint. The failed projected edit starts from original Gemma. Their weight displacements change 785,007 attention-output coefficients, 1,368,221 feed-forward-down coefficients and 31,552 coefficients, respectively. Their native parameter displacement norms are 0.02086, 0.008165 and 0.00006512. The first two are 320 and 125 times the failed edit’s norm. These are ordinary coefficient units across different families. Raw movement between the three model conditions can depend on the edit family, its size or its origin. Per-tensor rounded products [Successful generations and stored weights](../development/2026-11-17-descent-sign-swap/README.md) · [Failed candidate, native measurement and fresh arithmetic](../../studies/04-round-2-overlap/runs/directional-intervention-projected-native/README.md)

For a transition with unchanged architecture let its two local weight settings be A and B and its two incoming states be x and y. The direct change on the edited input is B(y) − A(y). The inherited change in the original map is A(y) − A(x). These add as vectors. Their norms need not add, since the vector changes can reinforce or cancel. An equally exact decomposition evaluated on x gives a different allocation when the local change depends on its input. The chosen order has a concrete interpretation here. It asks how the original transformation would handle the input the edited model actually received. Accuracy of the mixed endpoint A(y) is a separate numerical question. Arithmetic closure by construction cannot validate that endpoint.

The boundary being measured determines which inputs suffice. A post-attention output projection can take its native mixed value as a sufficient input for that projection. Isolating an entire attention transition requires the edited residual and attention context, including the causal input and the correct shared K/V producer. Pointwise feed-forward replay similarly distinguishes an intervention on the final down-projection at a fixed gated product from an intervention on the whole feed-forward block at a fixed entering residual. These definitions coincide for the direct forward effect when all the weights before the selected projection inside the transition are unchanged and the edited intermediate is retained.

## Did a direct local move distinguish descent repair?

At the symbolic prechoices, the primary signed result is the mean across the 105 merges of squared distance to the frozen successful reference at edited weights minus the same distance at original weights given the edited incoming states. Negative means the edit acted toward the reference locally. The two independently sufficient edits have opposite signs at both the descent and the friction computation. The wrong descent shares its sign with the successful attention edit. This directly falsifies the proposed common direction on this fitted set.

| Installed edit | Descent, signed local effect | Friction, signed local effect | Outside tolerance, descent | Outside tolerance, friction |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Successful attention transplant | +1.811 × 10⁻⁶ | +5.601 × 10⁻⁷ | 0/105 | 0/105 |
| Successful feed-forward transplant | −4.164 × 10⁻⁸ | −3.967 × 10⁻⁸ | 0/105 | 0/105 |
| Failed projected edit | +1.944 × 10⁻⁷ | +7.822 × 10⁻⁹ | 3/105 | 8/105 |

Independent reduction of the stored 105-merge arrays reproduced the table. At descent, 27 attention merges moved farther from the reference and 8 moved closer on the attention transplant. On the feed-forward transplant, 16 feed-forward merges moved farther and 19 moved closer. Each left its other 70 local maps exactly unchanged on their edited input. The two transplants change disjoint parameter families. A shared nonzero **direct** effect at the same local cell is therefore impossible by construction. A shared signed aggregate across the affected cells was a meaningful prediction and failed at these decisions. A common downstream effect delivered in the incoming state remains possible.

The failed model still correctly subtracted 13 J from the energy it produced. Its later numerical friction prechoice had 88/105 outside tolerance, at a matched event verified against its arithmetic audit. Holding inputs equal tells us whether a local map changed, but a larger absolute departure from a single reference can still accompany valid computation. The hinge had identically zero local change on both successful arms at both audited operations and their numerical restatements.
