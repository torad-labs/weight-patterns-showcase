## Tolerance evidence and admission

I independently read and SHA256-checked the original `chance.npy`: shape 560 × 105, exactly 1,365 zeros, exactly all merges at reference positions 0–12. Its positive minimum is 1.317790326434746e-11 and maximum is 1.9448615573776407. These are observations, not proposed thresholds.

For these zero cells, the original construction averages nonnegative squared distances and observed no variation. That identifies a zero empirical radius. It provides no positive amount of movement already demonstrated harmless. A later nonzero cell's variation cannot be assigned to these cells without an additional assumption relating the computations. Numerical reproducibility can bound numerical differences but cannot certify all acceptable computational movement. Enlarging until witnesses pass would also change the selection policy, and outcome-correct witnesses are not certified correct internally.

Consequently the current positive-allowance requirement is not scientifically determined by the available identical observations. A supported measurement/construction or an explicit reviewed change of the allowance policy is required; neither exists in the reviewed bindings. This does not prove that movement at these cells is harmful, or that a positive allowance is impossible. It states what the existing data identify. The loader's refusal of missing/unreviewed/nonpositive allowance is appropriate for this specification. Historical zero-radius loss remains historical evidence.
