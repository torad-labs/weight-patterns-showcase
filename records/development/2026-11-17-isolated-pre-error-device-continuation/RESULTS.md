## What happened at the selected computation

The prospective upper bound was 0.380180458. The original fitted projection was 0.393291497. Its excess of 0.013111040 rose to 0.015254537 after the standard update and to 0.013492804 after the directional update. Original and directional free generations mapped the reference position to current position 312. Standard generation mapped it to 325. The directional arm reduced excess relative to standard by 11.55%, while the 2.91% increase relative to its common starting state directly contradicts the expected local correction. Its capped whole-trace objective fell from 0.000292459960 to 0.000291613082.

For the original fitted acquisition, the extra selected-cell loss was 0.00000000292346, about 0.0010% of the standard whole-trace objective. This gives the scale of the added scalar term. The experiment did not isolate its weight derivative from all the other local derivatives, nor their respective contributions to the observed excess increase.

Both derivatives accounted for all 538 eligible parameter tensors. Each used the same empirical response multiplier. The resulting standard and directional step lengths were 0.00506289388223 and 0.00506284925709. Standard changed 31,553 stored values from the original and directional changed 31,564. Their installed arrays differed at **2,056 values across 84 tensors**, including 51 two-byte values across 23 tensors and 2,005 four-byte values across 61 tensors. The exact physically addressed bytes were verified on installation before generation.
