## Hypothesis and limitations

Preservation predicts that the frozen updated model still lies inside the original computational tolerance on the selected correct prompt, after the same-card original control is accounted for. A positive updated loss with a zero or smaller same-card original loss weakens preservation under this fixed metric. An altered history or correspondence is reported, not forced back. No fitted scale, tolerance floor, loss threshold, text judge or optimizer change follows automatically.
