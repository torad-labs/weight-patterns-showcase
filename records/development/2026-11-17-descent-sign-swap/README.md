Starting from the early model, **either later weight family on its own was sufficient to correct the descent sign, every downstream energy and the verdict** on this prompt. The attention-output transplant reproduced 99.84% of the full endpoint trace-loss decrease and exactly matched the later endpoint's entire generated history. The FFN-down transplant reproduced 99.21% and followed the later history through 323 choices, then used a colon in place of a period after explaining the climb. Its answer required the same calculation. All 105 merge losses were scored afresh for all four runs. On the early background 86 of 105 decreased under the attention-output transplant, and 85 of 105 decreased under the FFN-down transplant.

The first difference between the fresh early and late histories was generated index 72, consumed position 245. Both swaps followed the later choice at that first fork, which preceded their advances through the displayed calculation. The early control reproduced its historical subtraction and verdict using different wording and a different full history. Its fresh GPU1 loss was 0.00028457642 compared with 0.00130749396 in the historical acquisition. The late control reproduced its exact historical generated choices. All percentages above compare same-device conditions. [Four bound measurements, history comparisons and signed arithmetic](native-result.json).

The prediction that the FFN-down transplant would fix the arithmetic was borne out. Its companion prediction that the attention-only transplant would retain the old mistake was falsified. Adding the second later family after the first had repaired the case bought only 0.16% or 0.79% of the full trace improvement, depending on order. The positive loss-scale interaction was 0.00018544211. These are overlapping routes to this observed behavior. This experiment establishes sufficiency for each whole family in these backgrounds, without assigning the repair to a single weight, proving how often it generalizes, or locating the first repairing training update.
