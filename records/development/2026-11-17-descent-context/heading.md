# Does one early generated heading choice redirect the arithmetic?

At frozen checkpoint 175, giving each problem the other's full pre-sign framing reversed both freely generated sign choices. This follow-up changes only the earliest naturally divergent heading choice in the otherwise unchanged receiving answer. For both prompts, the model has already written the initial energy and started the descent section. At choice index 72, after the opening parenthesis, the fitted answer normally writes “before” and the development answer normally writes its true drop digit “1”. The alternative for the fitted case is its own true drop digit “2”. The alternative for the development case is “before”.

Every generated choice through index 71 comes from that receiver's own natural history. The intervention supplies only its alternative at index 72. The label for the energy at the bottom, the first operation, its signed numerical derivation, and all later quantities remain free. Raw receipts retain every choice and compare all 73 full vocabulary score hashes, including the as-yet unforced distribution at the manipulated index, to the matched natural acquisition. The earlier full-prefix experiment qualified both same-device controls. Two prospective intervention prefixes.

## One heading choice induced the error and failed to rescue it

All 73 native score hashes through the manipulated heading choice matched the original same-device history on each question. Each case then generated the rest of the solution freely.

| Problem | One altered heading choice | Free symbolic rule | Free numerical calculation | Available / required | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fitted | “before” → “2” | Initial kinetic energy minus a positive descent magnitude | 64 − 40 = 24 J | 11 J / 20 J | NO |
| Unfitted | “1” → “before” | Initial kinetic energy plus the signed change in potential energy | 96 + (−30) = 66 J | 55 J / 30 J | YES |

For the fitted question, changing the heading choice alone was sufficient to turn a physically correct solution into the observed sign error. The model generated the heading “State after Descent (2 m)”, the label for kinetic energy at the bottom, the positive gain 40 J and the subtractive operation. Its margin for addition over subtraction at the symbolic operation moved from +6.031 naturally to −2.213.

For the unfitted question the changed heading was insufficient. It freely completed “State after Descent (before rough section)”. It then defined the *change in potential energy*, correctly calculated ΔPE = −30 J, and wrote initial kinetic energy **plus** ΔPE. For conservation on a smooth descent it should have subtracted the signed change, giving 96 − (−30) = 126 J. Its explicit numerical expression instead gave 96 − 30 = 66 J. The free symbolic plus at index 139 had a +3.523 logit advantage over minus; the numerical minus at index 154 had a 13.952 logit advantage over plus. A positive operator count would therefore misclassify this intervention as a repair. Its final YES was compatible with both 55 J and the correct 115 J available.

The four-cell full-prefix experiment supplied a positive descent magnitude and the receiving case's correct operands beside the alternative quantity label; that longer context repaired the unfitted calculation. The two experiments together show an asymmetric role for the first heading choice and a causal effect of the longer preceding context.
