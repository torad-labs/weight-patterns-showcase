## Shared diagnosis of recorded decode

The purpose is to let Marcos and an AI investigate the same recorded events and discover performance opportunities. The mechanical world supplies spatial context; a synchronized worker timeline and evidence inspector supply measured intervals, record identity and their limits. Diagnosis is an operating mode within the existing visual world.

**Diagnose recording** opens the worker timeline beside the machine. Choose a worker, operation or recorded-order link to inspect the same selection at every depth. Time-window controls magnify the recording, layer or operation without changing playback time. Observed exit distributions suggest places to investigate; they never claim saved time or a complete critical path. Share links restore the exact inspection, and evidence JSON plus `window.decodeInspection.snapshot()` expose the same selection to an AI. See `DIAGNOSTICS.md` for scope and evidence semantics.

The original capture measures thread-0 stamp arrivals. Program-order links cannot establish the producer responsible for a wait. Transfers, cache outcomes and warp strokes remain illustrations. Capture and build provenance, missing measurements and the original semantics accompany exported evidence. No inference instrumentation changes are included in this first diagnostic extension.

The evidence inspector tells the investigation as connected visual stories: a worker's chronological strip, operation relationships, worker arrival clusters, and matching-operation ranges across the recordings. Architecture branches lead from observations through competing explanations to distinguishing measurements and conditional experiments. Its relational export preserves the selected branch and links every derived measurement to its inputs. The [evidence ontology](EVIDENCE-ONTOLOGY.md) describes this shared vocabulary and the NVIDIA-backed investigation method.

**Follow the reason** extends that inspection with eight selectable relationships: prerequisite readiness, instruction supply, useful versus issued work, buffer residence, time-resolved supply and activity, value distributions, token trajectories and the instruments behind them. Imported observations bind to the selected capture and operation. Authored examples occupy a separately labeled mode with their own reversible clock. Missing hardware observations remain visible gaps. The same complete evidence bundle and links are available to an AI through the inspection export. [PROFILING-LENSES.md](PROFILING-LENSES.md) defines the scope and capture contract.
