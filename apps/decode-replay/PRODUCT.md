# Recorded decode world

QGRE's operator needs to see how a recorded token traverses the actual layer mechanisms and where recorded work or waits occupy time. The product reads existing phase tapes, Nsight captures and source composition; it never evaluates a model or invents internal timings.

The primary experience is a navigable, videogame-like scene, not a report. The operator can move between connected layers, zoom into their substeps, play the complete token, and compare two mechanisms side by side without scrolling between them. Prose belongs in optional evidence detail. A compact transport, layer navigation and timing directly on parts stay available.

Success: both saved E2B tapes traverse all 35 layers; every recorded step is reachable; selecting, opening, comparing and returning retain the one recording's clock. Shared-span and untimed anatomy are visibly different from independent measurements. The scene fills the 3840×2050 desk viewport, with no stacked dashboard above it; smaller screens retain navigation and inspection.

Local/offline only. No uploads, new GPU captures, fabricated utilization, physical SM placement claims or tensor simulation. Invalid imports preserve the prior working world.
