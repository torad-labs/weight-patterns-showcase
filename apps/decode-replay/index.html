Bring a recorded decode into view

Generate this page from your source checkout and existing captures. Nothing is uploaded and no GPU work is launched.

qgre dev decode-replay --output runs/legs/replay/my-decode \
  --nsys capture.nsys-rep --ncu capture.ncu-rep \
  --phase-tape phase-report.json

Open the generated index.html directly, or load its bundle.json above. Nsight Systems records launches; a phase tape exposes instrumented work inside the megakernel. NCU metrics stay attached to their own recording, not guessed positions in another trace.
