The recorded decision gives percentage reductions value as reporting milestones. On the checkpoints tested, reducing the fitted loss farther did not supply better reasoning on the unfitted problem. Choose a repair recipe using the earliest evaluated setting that sustains correct fitted arithmetic, preserves a known correct computation, and fixes the unfitted intermediate error. Keep fresh loss, stability and validated arithmetic visible separately. The original development control distributions overlapped, so there is no calibrated universal threshold.
