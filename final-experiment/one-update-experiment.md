## Post-freeze transfer analysis

The unchanged transfer control exposed two separable limitations. Under the frozen computational correspondence, reference row 312 matched transfer row 296, where the model was already writing an equals sign after the descent multiplication. The original model chose subtraction at row 259. Its actual pre-sign computation was at row 258, 38 rows before the matched position, and corresponded to reference row 261. The axis projection was 0.14120 at the matched row and 0.27370 immediately before the wrong sign. Both are within the frozen allowance of 0.38018. All 93 earlier decoded positions also fall below the allowance, with a maximum of 0.29950 at row 257 for the original and 0.29940 for the directional arm. Moving the comparison to the analogous point alone therefore would not make this axis identify the original transfer error. Reading positions relative to the sign is a post-freeze diagnostic. The experiment's training correspondence, cap and update remain as declared. This November 17 result weakens the proposed single-cell correction under the declared one-update rule. The fitted request was active and the edited computation moved globally closer to the reference, while the selected cell moved farther outside the allowance and the arithmetic stayed wrong. The held case exposes issues both with finding the corresponding computation and with carrying the same direction into a different valid setup. A different scale or signal would be a separate prospective test. The existing cohort result remains evidence of a directional precursor in those development traces.
