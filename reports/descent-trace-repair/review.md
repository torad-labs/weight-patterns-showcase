## Trace and learner review

The four prior reports describe different measurements. The [single-pair study](../fixing-a-reasoning-mistake-by-editing-the-weights.pdf) (§§4–7) selects one matrix and changes its response to recorded inputs. The [trace guide](../how-the-trace-derived-edit-works.pdf) (§§4–8) measures the change in normalized direction from incoming to merged stream, fits selected climb-anchor positions and shows how this support misses errors elsewhere. The [many-trace study](../how-the-path-edit-works.pdf) (equations 1–5 and §§6–9) registers variable-length responses by their turns. Its installed edit targets post-merge state directions. The [iterative proposal](../Iterative_Learning_from_Computational_Traces.docx) proposes local tolerance, current-history differentiation, regeneration, and measuring preservation and end-to-end cost. The [Final Experiment](../../FINAL-EXPERIMENT.md) defines the later single-pair loss. Its execution update is in the [living experiment record](../../final-experiment/training-and-stopping.md).

For every paired position the baseline measures the difference of unit incoming and outgoing stream directions at all 105 merges. It computes the vector difference from the fixed original reference, removes the calibrated local radius, weights the remaining vector by the square root of represented pair mass and sums its squared norm. Some original calibration radii are zero. A single scalar reduction retains distinct derivatives for the different local residual vectors. Current-history VJP propagates this loss through a Torch graph anchored to the native states, into 538 eligible parameter arrays in the full-weight phase. The directional response determines step length with a calibrated multiplier. Native BF16/F32 storage with FP32 remainder determines the actual change. This is differentiation of a reconstructed continuous computation along a held discrete history. It cannot predict a changed greedy history through that derivative alone. Neither observation identifies the first causal update or isolates the effect of intervening on block 33 alone.

The frozen comparisons use no sealed cohort. The discussion now acknowledges the fourteen successful traces already used for calibration before explaining what multiple-example fitting would test.
