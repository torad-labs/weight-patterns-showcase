## What supplies a weight update

One wrong response and one fixed original reference supply the measured learner with two sequences of internal computations. At each captured event, the native model exposes the residual entering and leaving each of three merges in 35 blocks. Each turn is the outgoing unit vector minus the incoming unit vector. This retains the change in direction at a merge. Positive rescaling of either endpoint leaves that turn unchanged. Equality of turns therefore leaves some physical transformations indistinguishable. The 105-merge alignment compares the concatenated turns at entire events by cosine, then minimizes the summed distance along a full-coverage monotone path. It allows several partners for one event. It uses no arithmetic annotation, sign token, chosen word, or manually specified operation boundary. It establishes a correspondence under these features, without identifying the causal meaning of a pair. [Final Experiment, §§3–6](../../FINAL-EXPERIMENT.md) · [Alignment](../../code/signal-trace/pipeline/overlap/complete_overlap.py)

The local residual is the vector displacement beyond the fixed radius of that reference event and merge. Multiplicity splits each reference cell’s mass among its matches. Squaring and summing gives a scalar loss while preserving the different derivatives of individual cells. The derivative of a turn includes its two endpoints. Reverse differentiation sums the effects of shared eligible weights across the full trace. The controller derives its step length from the first-order response along that direction, applies the recorded empirical multiplier, predicts native rounding and installs the checked bytes with their carried remainders. The 538 arrays describe eligible learned parameters. A zero derivative, an unused embedding row or rounding can leave a coefficient unchanged. The next loss comes from a fresh acquisition against the same original reference, after the newly installed weights generate the response. The derivative conditions on the acquired history and alignment, and uses a straight-through approximation to the native precision operations. It does not differentiate through an alternative future generation. [Local residual and weights](../../code/signal-trace/pipeline/weight_edit/vector_chance.py) · [Measured recovery controller](../../records/development/2026-11-17-unmeasured-state-recovery/run.py)

The original reports describe several distinct interventions. The first edits used a paired, rank-one change. The trace-derived guide describes local matrix fits. The many-trace path report differentiates a normalized post-merge state through branch normalization, residual addition and the matrix input in equations 4–7. The iterative-learning proposal’s repair equation sums local squared excesses. Its other displayed terms include preservation, behavior and a penalty on moving the weights. Its cost inequality requires setup and update costs at comparable achieved performance. These constructions motivate the present method. They cannot supply an unmeasured transfer rate, a complete causal description of a capability, or a compute advantage for this implementation. ## Which decisions the two stopping implementations make

The whole-trace selector reads the fitted trajectory and separately registered development prompts at scheduled checkpoints. It measures complete regenerated computations with the same turn alignment and per-cell excess function, against the fitted reference or a separately declared whole reference. At each development baseline it takes an unchanged-weight repeat. It measures the full objective and the mass of loss on coordinates where *all* baseline partners were inside tolerance. It promotes an endpoint when at least one of those measurements improves beyond its repeat range and none worsens beyond its range. It can stop on repeated deterioration of retained mass, two intervals without resolved development gain, incomplete evaluation, or exhaustion of the allowance. It records capability and durability as unmeasured. No arithmetic label, operation-specific region, or task-specific scorer supplies this decision. The baseline mask is fixed, and its retention statistic sums across cells. A gain at one previously departed cell can offset a regression at another. Neither zero repeat range on two deterministic decodes nor a better panel trace establishes a guaranteed improvement in physical reasoning. [Whole-trace policy](../../code/signal-trace/pipeline/weight_edit/trace_stopping.py)

The optional physics selector runs after an installed checkpoint has been measured. Its inputs are additional development decodes, predefined reference cells for each named operation, calibration measurements and externally adjudicated arithmetic. The driver aligns the entire trace computationally, then reads only the preselected cells for the geometry of each operation. An operation qualifies when its arithmetic is right and its distance lies within its calibrated range. Correct arithmetic outside that range gets its own receipt. Either arithmetic or geometric regression can end a run after two scheduled observations. A first joint closure ends immediately. A finite update allowance and lack of observable progress can also end it. Selection among attained checkpoints therefore depends on the supplied labels and reference cells. The derivatives and the installed step proposed at a given checkpoint receive none of them. This is task-specific *model selection*. No frozen checkpoint in the comparison was trained or chosen using this optional selector. [Optional policy](../../code/signal-trace/pipeline/weight_edit/validation_stopping.py) · [Policy’s acquisition and calibration adapter](../../code/signal-trace/pipeline/weight_edit/validation_driver.py)

The 11-update interval follows the longest recovered fitted dry spell plus one in a particular 42-update calibration trajectory. This gives an observable procedure on a development set. It measures neither a minimum future recovery time nor stable acquisition on independent configurations. A panel with unavailable arithmetic or missing geometry receives an inconclusive termination. A conditional lower bound from a fixed alignment gives a different piece of information: the selected reference balls may already be mutually incompatible even for freely movable turns. The bound changes with the alignment. Neither result identifies a universal percentage at which training should stop.
