## What the paper does not claim

No repair of a general capability beyond the fitted family; no transfer rate for reordered routes; no compute-cost comparison against fine-tuning or RL; no claim that the 28 transfer questions were absent from pretraining. The sealed `reserved-40b` stays closed.
