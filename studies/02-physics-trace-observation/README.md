# Study Phase 02: Pre-Edit Physics Trace Observation

Executed November 6–7, 2026 on **Gemma 4 E2B** through an instrumented standalone `llama.cpp` runner, these four pre-edit studies establish the mechanical energy-conservation benchmark used throughout Phases 03 and 04, qualify a bit-exact residual-stream recorder in both `Q4_K_M` and native `BF16`, characterize a recurring mid-network transformation segment across matched prompt pairs, and test a one-step activation transplant that exposes Gemma 4 E2B's shared-KV persistence boundary.

## Architectural Takeaway Leading to Phase 03

`physics-intervention` established a critical structural constraint of Gemma 4 E2B (`gemma4.cpp`):
- **Layers `0–14`** write new Key/Value cache entries.
- **Layers `15–34`** share K/V caches written by layers `13` (sliding) and `14` (full attention).

Because a one-time activation injection at layer `16` lies downstream of every K/V writer, its perturbation cannot persist into future decoding steps unless it immediately flips the discrete token emitted on that exact step. In contrast, a **persistent weight edit** at the same layer re-executes on every subsequent token's forward pass—directly motivating the Round 1 persistent weight-edit ladder in [../03-round-1-single-pair-edits/README.md](../03-round-1-single-pair-edits/README.md).
