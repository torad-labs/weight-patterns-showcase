# Predictions fixed after discovery, before confirmation

The confirmation responses and captures do not exist when this document is written. The two withheld families are cities and materials, 20 cases total. The selected inspection locations remain layer 29/head 5, layer 27/head 7, and layer 24/head 3 (zero-based). No other head is substituted if a prediction fails.

These are small descriptive predictions about observable expressions of a working capability. They do not define or identify Marcos's proposed frozen encoded path. All internal claims require exact answer-token agreement across the ordinary server, native plain run, and native capture run. Missing or ineligible cases are reported, never silently counted as successes.

1. **Behavior and observation agreement:** all 20 ordinary answers will match the requested value, position, alternate value, or copied word; all 20 answer-token sequences will agree across the three execution modes.
2. **Question-dependent affinity:** at each selected head, all eight matched value/position pairs will have greater pre-cache diagnostic affinity mass on the requested body value for the value question. The requested value will receive more than half of the affinity allocated to the three body values in all ten value/alternate questions. Both absolute mass and the conditional share must be reported.
3. **Stable descriptive weight/input products:** at each selected head, the mean product-profile cosine for cross-content value questions will exceed the matched value-versus-position mean. Changing table to prose, or changing row order, will each yield a larger mean cosine than changing value to position. Pair counts are correlated observations, not independent statistical samples.
4. **Copy overlap remains:** at each selected head, the mean value-versus-copy product cosine will exceed the value-versus-position mean, and mean affinity on the body value in copy prompts will exceed the position-question mean. This predicts an unresolved specificity problem, not a successful lookup detector.
5. **Input-only comparison:** at each selected layer, the captured input vectors themselves will also have greater mean cross-content value-task cosine than matched value-versus-position cosine. If so, the product-profile contrast cannot by itself be credited to discovery of a distinctive organization of weight bits.

No numeric threshold is chosen after looking at confirmation. Predictions use the inequalities above, not an optimized classifier. Results of each clause and each selected head will be retained.

## Interpretation limits already visible in discovery

- 40/40 discovery answers are correct and agree across the three modes, but the first-token logit arrays differ between plain and capture: RMS difference averages 0.4102 (range 0.0144–1.7005); maximum absolute difference reaches 3.8499. Answer agreement establishes observed behavioral stability on these questions, not numerical transparency of instrumentation.
- Value versus position changes instruction wording and word/number output type. Copy is only sampled in the canonical table layout. These contrasts do not remove every confound.
- Product profiles are offline signed sums of native-decoded stored weights times captured input, in observer-chosen groups of 32 input coordinates. Native formats are Q4_K at layer 29 and Q6_K at layers 27 and 24. They are not bit-identical GPU arithmetic, bit-level attribution, or an inferred distributed path.
- The plain model is never changed. No training, intervention, or edit is part of this experiment.
