# The work is deriving a reliable training configuration

Marcos's clarification to Astra, November 11, 2026 (CT). This records the purpose of the current work and the distinction Astra was asked to maintain with the research seats. It introduces no law, hook, gate, numerical threshold or change to the method's steps.

## What Marcos wants

Marcos asked Astra to hold the line. There is evidence the technique works: it was reached several times in several different ways, and there are hypotheses. Just as reinforcement learning needs the right parameters, configuration and derivation to work, the evidence says this is the phase the research is in. Fable often keeps going in the direction that the technique is not proven and does not work, but the evidence says otherwise, so Astra is to keep everyone in check.

## What that means for this work

The earlier repairs and transfer are positive evidence already obtained, through multiple constructions. The unresolved task is deriving the parameters, configuration, optimizer and measurement procedure that reliably reproduce, combine and preserve those gains. It is not repeatedly reopening whether the technique has ever worked.

A particular controlled sweep can fail its declared bounds, restoration, replay or own-answer acceptance. That failure is reported honestly and precisely. It does not erase the earlier successful repairs or justify the blanket statement that the technique does not work. “This controlled configuration has not yet produced an accepted complete sweep” and “the technique has not worked” are different claims.

Conversely, the earlier successes do not mean every configuration succeeds or that reliable controlled training is already finished. The task is to explain each concrete failure at the level the evidence supports, then derive a better construction—not to hide negative results or assume a cause without examining it.

Astra is responsible for keeping that distinction explicit in objective and acceptance decisions and in discussion with the solver and performance seats. This also applies to Astra's own wording: the earlier framing that treated possibility as the unresolved question was corrected. New results should build on what the diary has already established, not reset the research to zero after each unsuccessful configuration.

The research method, original-only calibration, actual-BF16 evaluation and honest reporting of failures remain in force. Reserved-40b remains sealed. No new enforcement machinery follows from this clarification.
