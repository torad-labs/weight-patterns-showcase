# The loss must follow shared computation, not a recorded wording

November 15, 2026 (CT). Marcos's direction was relayed by claude-research at
8:48 and 8:50 AM CT. Astra owns the objective and the decisions below.

## What is wrong

The current loss compares a current row with one assigned success's recorded
row, reached through a correspondence to the recipient's original history. Its
tolerance is the spread of original successful discrepancies. That does not
establish the computation the successful answers actually share at that point.
When the generated history changes, word-dependent changes in the relation and
changes in the assigned correspondence can become loss even where no recurring
capability discrepancy has been identified.

The loop does regenerate and register its own trace each step. What it does not
do is establish, in that overlap, which local computation the correct traces
agree on and where the current path departs from that shared computation. A
complete monotonic alignment to fixed rows is not that agreement. An envelope
that grants every original success zero is not that agreement either.

Performance locates the missing instrument in `c141fafd`: the Torch loop uses
`torch_forward.registered` and native acquisition uses
`capture_bound.laid_on_original` to align each rewritten answer with its own
original recording, including a wrong answer with its own original wrong trace.
Correct traces enter only through the previously assigned mate rows. At the stop, no existing
step instrument directly overlapped the current answer with the correct traces.
Performance subsequently built the direct instrument (`8c1740c1`, 9:10 AM CT),
assessed in `records/retrospectives/2026-11-15-direct-overlap-assessment-astra.md`:
that missing correspondence now exists, but the local shared-agreement definition
and an admitted correction loop do not.

After one update, the original outcome groups are poorly separated by this
reading. Fable's sampled changed-history tables report pair-ordering shares
roughly 0.34–0.60, compared with 0.8578 in the original-history nonself diagnostic.
The latter uses a different calibration, and the four original-weight controls
use another card/anchor; those limitations remain. The paired first-step control
reported later is about 1.0e-08 apart across the two combinations, but it does not
measure that difference at every later state. None of these caveats resolves the
known reference problem: the objective can penalize different expression of a
computation rather than its divergence from what the successful traces share.

The directional turn contains dependence on the input being processed. Comparing
its absolute direction with one recorded mate does not separate that dependence
from the shared capability computation. The current records do not establish how
much of the changed-history loss is each contribution. They also do not establish
which regenerated answers are repaired or damaged. Neither unknown licenses
continuing to call this the measured capability divergence.

## The step that is required

From each reached model state, generate its answers again, overlap the whole
current traces with the original correct traces by computation, identify the
locally shared correct computation, measure departure from it, and take the
correction on that divergence. In the same overlap, observe whether the originally
correct paths have begun diverging. Different words do not themselves supply a
correction request. Where the correct traces do not agree, this construction has
not established a reference level of one; it must not manufacture one from a
single mate or count a missing reference as demonstrated closure.

All original rows and merges remain visible. This is not permission to crop to a
sign, hand-pick a stretch, declare every successful answer flawless, or retune a
reference until an edited score is favorable. Original reference data and fitting,
no-fit and reserved roles remain unchanged.

## What is known, and what is not yet solved

Known: the fixed mate and pooled original envelope do not establish local shared
agreement. Regeneration alone does not repair that definition. The same-history
reading is useful sensitivity evidence, but changing weights on a supplied history
and changing the computation by supplying a new history are different questions.

Not yet solved: an executable definition that extracts shared correct computation
across histories while retaining the recurring error signal and legitimate
alternative routes. The current control has not been evaluated against such a
reference. Nor has it been evaluated under a newly specified amount-only objective
that retains turn amount and gain without stream direction. No values for either
are claimed here, and no new calculation is started to postpone this diagnosis.

Existing original-model recordings in several wordings, including the two cards,
fed continuations and opening transplants, provide source material for assessing
that definition. Constant weights alone do not prove an intervention preserved
correct computation: the overlap must distinguish a change of wording from a
change in the computational path. No arbitrary similarity cutoff, favorable
subset, new outcome grader or automatic reference selection is specified here.
The next work is the definition and its assessment on existing evidence, not more
steps under the present loss.

## Source-only assessment after the stop

Fable reports `records/development/2026-11-15-relation-reading/token-coincidence.json`
from the original's own texts and existing direct original pairs: 0.877 of the
envelope loss lies at different-input-token pairs, and successful pairs' joint
turn/gain distances are 7.3 times larger there in layers 0–14 and 9.0 times larger
in layers 15–34. These are accepted descriptive findings, not recomputed here.
They establish sensitivity before rewriting, so removing the second-hand current
correspondence alone does not resolve the reference. They do not establish that
0.877 of the loss is irrelevant wording: token differences can accompany real
computational differences.

The amount-only descriptor remains a candidate, not an admitted objective. Let
`u(x) = x / ||x||`, turn amount `a = ||u(y) - u(x)||`, and gain
`g = log(||y|| / ||x||)`. For a unit input `x = e1`, the two unit outputs
`y+ = cos(theta)e1 + sin(theta)e2` and
`y- = cos(theta)e1 - sin(theta)e2` have the same amount and gain but different
outgoing directions. A fixed downstream read along `e2` distinguishes them. This
is an algebraic counterexample to sufficiency, not a measured case in this model
or evidence against amounts carrying a useful partial capability signal.

Dropping direction from the score also does not drop it from the correspondence:
the capture's path search still compares directional turns at all 105 merges.
An amount-only reading on those pairs measures amounts **conditional on that
alignment**, not wording independence of the complete measurement. No change to
that alignment is prescribed by this observation. Likewise, sharing the same
weights or original outcome label does not certify identical correct computation
on two histories.

Performance's source-only direct-overlap instrument removes the detour through a
recipient's original trace. Its fifteen original-success frames, direct current
and nonself-success pairs, witness identities, directional relations, amounts and
gains retain the evidence needed to define local agreement. The table itself does
not define agreement, and a forced correspondence is not independent support.
The remaining definition must identify what relation the original witnesses share
and how a current trace's departure is measured, without turning unsuccessful
comparison or incompatible reference routes into either a correction request or
zero loss. All rows remain accounted for; unsupported reference cells are unknown,
not closed. No pooled centre, self-inclusive envelope, new threshold or amount-only
loss is selected here. This asks for an explicit, testable construction, not proof
of a perfect target before experimentation.

Fable's amount-only and same-question/different-wording readings are separate work;
no result for them is claimed by this assessment, and Astra does not duplicate them.
No model execution, trace calculation or replacement declaration was performed for
this assessment.
