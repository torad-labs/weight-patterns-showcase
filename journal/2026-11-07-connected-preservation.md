# What candidate C adds

Marcos asked whether this direction was becoming a loose end. He sees recurring evidence across the experiments and wants to turn that understanding into edits that preserve other behavior. He also shared a possible future connection to more efficient RL, explicitly asking us not to investigate it now.

We kept the research object: a context-dependent information-transformation trace through many weights. The observations identify partial contributions; the experiment does not require recovering one self-contained physics function or assigning knowledge to individual scalar entries.

B showed that preserving a similar correction at one input did not preserve its development later. C preserves the actual stored A action across304 connected inputs through two observed repairs, while reducing it at nine inputs associated with the known damaged branch. The candidate is a stored BF16 matrix change and ordinary native generation uses it throughout inference.

C passed the declared conjunction: both repairs survive with A-identical continuations; the 4 kg continuation returns exactly to original; all seven prior correct chains remain identical. It also retains the earlier disc4 numerical repair. The fresh four physics prompts do not improve. The corrected numerical answers still coexist with prose errors, and the restored4kg final triple still has original intermediate errors.

My assessment is that this is useful progress toward selective edits guided by connected observations. The primary result is on known cases and near-tie greedy decisions; it does not yet establish transfer, broad stability, or a full account of knowledge distribution. The RL possibility remains a future hypothesis, with no investigation or training performed.
