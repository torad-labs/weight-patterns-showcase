# Overlap of connected capability traces

Marcos's clarification, November 7, 2026 (CT).

The proposed observable is the **computational trace across many weights while a response develops**. Asking varied questions that require the same capability can expose recurring portions when those executions are aligned and compared. The pattern is in how the information changes through connected operations. It is not a collection of large weights, an average activation, a read vector, or merely the fact that every question uses the same architecture.

“Overlap” preserves the progression: the incoming context, the transformation, its downstream contribution, and the continuation. Different requests can express the same useful contribution with different values, token lengths, and intermediate states. We seek a partial recurring influence, without demanding a complete isolated physics function or an exclusive signature.

The existing theory document already states this. The experimental implementation had not yet reached the proposed many-trace repair: A used one paired input/output difference; B and C changed preservation; D changed the background sensitivity of the read. Those are useful intervention results, but none should be relabeled as a repair direction discovered by overlapping many matched failing/correct trajectories.

The next study therefore separates three claims:

1. **Observed recurrence:** corresponding transformations develop together across related executions, with the actual computational connections retained.
2. **Repair proposal:** those contrasts supply a candidate persistent weight change, with background sensitivity and existing successful trajectories constrained.
3. **Behavioral result:** ordinary generation on unused questions improves, remains unchanged, or regresses. Teacher-forced answers are observations for construction, never counted as model successes.

The first incorrect calculation defines a failure group. A correct final verdict does not erase an earlier incorrect energy calculation. A formula error, arithmetic error, and wrong comparison are kept distinct; different energy-reference mistakes retain their subtypes. Success/failure contrasts are also conditioned on different generated histories after they branch, which must remain visible in the evidence.

For this study, we retain D's background second-moment preconditioning and trajectory preservation while extending the construction to multiple observed inputs. D's single read vector is an intervention handle, not the capability itself. We do not require the intended recurring trace to peak only on printed numbers: an earlier contextual contribution may matter. Localization is measured against matched controls rather than assumed from token appearance.
