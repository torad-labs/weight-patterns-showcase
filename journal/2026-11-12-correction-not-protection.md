## What the work is now

The hypothesis remains the recurring discrepancy in the connected computation of correct and wrong answers, and repairing that discrepancy through the weights. Earlier repairs and transfer are evidence already established; the configuration is being corrected, not the hypothesis abandoned.

There is one training objective: correct the computation toward the correct overlap, including size. Where the current computation already agrees, its discrepancy supplies no correction or only the small correction warranted by what the traces show. Where it diverges, the discrepancy supplies the correction. Agreement is read again as changes propagate; it is not an immutable mask taken from the first state.

There is no separate preservation objective, protection region, bound-feasibility training goal or bringing-back phase in the new construction. Renaming the existing protection penalties as correction would not implement this instruction. The earlier phone wording that protection belongs at overlaps was the phone session's interpretation, subsequently withdrawn, not Marcos's instruction.

Progress is the computation gap closed over the whole model, on the model's own trace. Written matrices, locally reduced loss and satisfied historical bounds are not substitutes. The answers remain evidence of the capability alongside the trace, not the training driver.

The phone session later clarified that its initial proposed implementation/check was its interpretation of the goal, not additional instructions from Marcos. The direct corrections above remain the authority for the intent. The team chooses the construction and experiment below in its own domain.
