# Two continuing research fronts

Decision agreed with Marcos, November 7, 2026 (CT). This is the continuing original objective, not a newly requested theory.

## Single-pair investigation

Use a successful/failing pair to inspect a particular departure and derive a persistent edit from an observed contribution. Follow its downstream effects and evaluate other cases. A began this way; B changed selectivity, C preserved connected passages, and D adjusted sensitivity to background inputs. The pair can reveal a conditional contribution that aggregation across many responses obscures.

This front remains active. Its earlier repairs, regression, preservation and limits are retained. A's correction came from one paired projection at prediction 235; the earlier recurring observations guided the region of investigation. Do not turn that history into either “no trace work happened” or “the repair direction was already extracted from many completions.”

## Comparison across connected traces

Use relevant questions that exercise the same capability to inspect how transformations develop, recur and depart. Preserve the incoming context, actual computational connections, outgoing contribution and continuation. Numerical values and lengths may differ. Find a useful partial recurring influence; exclusivity is not required.

Use that observed progression to determine an edit. Selecting positions by recurrence and then fitting every full activation difference at those positions does not by itself isolate the recurring contribution. A proposed construction must explain which observed part determined it. Averages, cosine similarities and a shared architecture are measurements or controls, not automatic capability identities.

## How the fronts inform each other

Maintain separate construction histories and a common comparison table. Single-pair findings can suggest what to inspect across executions; recurring findings can send us back to an individual pair. Record that exchange so shared data are not described as independent confirmation. A disagreement may reveal a conditional branch, a correspondence error, or different successful procedures. Neither method is presumed superior.

[Round two](../studies/04-round-2-overlap/ROUND-2.md) keeps the original checkpoint, engine, prompts, sampling and evaluation conditions where they permit the intended measurement. The recorder must expand to cover the signal's progress through the whole model. Edit location, number of matrices and edit form are outcomes of trace comparison, not fixed historical parameters. Native precision remains part of the experimental identity.

## Construction limits and ownership

The assistant introduced C's four-times-A norm ceiling, then retained it for D/E. It was an experiment-size choice, not a user-specified limit or an established stability threshold. E's post-fit scale of 1/256 left only about 0.18244% target-error reduction. That limits what E tested.

Do not silently inherit that ceiling as the purpose of the next experiment. State any proposed size constraint and its rationale, report intended and realized changes, and examine a construction that demands a very large edit. Larger is not presumed better or safe. Weight changes remain separate saved candidates; background effects, preservation and held-out outcomes are measured.
