## The idea

We want to recognize the trace of information being transformed while a model
exercises a capability. Each participating weight contributes to a small part of
that unfolding. Its effect depends on the incoming signal and the preceding and
surrounding computation. The recurring pattern may be in how connected changes
develop together, even when individual values, participating components, and
routes vary.

Marcos calls this a residual trace: observable effects of an influence whose full
mechanism may remain hidden. "Residual" is not restricted to the Transformer's
residual stream. "Horizontal" means distributed participation across many
weights, with overlap among capabilities.

**Recognizing a useful fragment is already a result.** We do not first need a
complete physics function, a uniquely isolated capability signature, a recovered
hidden variable, a closed geometric pattern, or the ability to reproduce the
whole behavior. A fragment can contribute to several capabilities.

## How we could make it observable

Shape requests so a particular capability, or a few related capabilities,
contributes strongly and repeatedly. Vary the circumstances while preserving its
use. The questions can supply some of the narrowing that a small specialized
network's limited task provides.

Follow connected transformations through each execution. Compare how they unfold
across relevant successes and characteristic failures. Recurring portions may
make a useful progression, departure, or return toward a loop legible enough to
investigate a repair. This is "completing the dots." No specific extraction or
editing formula has been selected.

Numbers record what happened. Searching isolated magnitudes, similar disconnected
weights, common active components, or a layer itinerary does not establish the
pattern. "Overlap" does not mean blindly averaging raw traces. Preserve actual
dependencies and context, and leave gaps visible.

## Why Marcos believes the examples connect

- **EDC CPT:** the same factual content recurs through varied phrasing; training
  reinforces its accessibility, while varied later uses may expose its influence.
- **RL:** useful procedures recur across different successful completions and may
  become more reliably expressed. This is not a claim of equal gradients.
- **Small-network recognition:** constrained tasks can make functional
  organization more legible, sometimes only partially.
- **Physics/control repair:** enough context can identify how a sequence needs
  to develop to succeed. The published car repair added a conditional control
  path; it did not establish recovery of a complete physics function.
- **Game-policy construction and Go:** known tasks can guide coordinated weight
  construction; observations can expose where intended behavior fails.
- **Qwen:** varied problems exposed a recurring loop tendency. Altering a
  repetition-associated contribution improved behavior without reconstructing the
  entire reasoning capability. Branch pruning is an analogy for this effect.

The forest analogy is central: changing stars or a lake may guide different
travelers along different routes, yet their marks can reveal recurring influence
without revealing the cue or the complete navigation method. The hidden-z
analogy likewise concerns observing effects without isolating their cause.
Other analogies are explanatory tools, not literal Transformer mechanisms.

## Where the work stands

This is a motivated hypothesis, not a completed local discovery or a guaranteed
safe editing method. The earlier frozen-pattern-only account is superseded.
Historical lookup captures and weight-similarity results are not retroactively
evidence that this trace was found.

The direction is capability-focused observation without training. Use the local
llama.cpp fork for this research line. Honor the actual quantization in captures
and any later realized edit. No new run or edit is requested merely by this
handoff. Preserve the distinction between Marcos's hypothesis, the evidence, and
the analogies, while addressing the proposition he actually made.
