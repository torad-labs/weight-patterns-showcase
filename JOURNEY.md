## Chapter 1: Orienting in Weight & Activation Space (Nov 4–6)

The project began in [`studies/01-early-exploration/`](studies/01-early-exploration/) by asking how far direct inspection of weights and single-pass activations could go on Gemma 4 E2B (`35` layers, residual width `1536`, shared KV caches from layers `13/14` into `15–34`, GeGLU MLPs up to width `12288`, and per-layer-input gates):

- **Long-horizon decode characterization:** [`decode-observatory`](studies/01-early-exploration/decode-observatory/run/ANALYSIS.md) captured 30,000 generated tokens across 187 multi-turn steps (decode latency slope $2.725\text{ ms}$ per $1{,}000$ context tokens, $R^2 = 0.999525$), while [`apps/decode-machine/`](apps/decode-machine/) was built to inspect cross-layer signals visually rather than through summary scalars.
- **Separating runtime artifacts from model mechanisms:** [`llamacpp-lookup-comparison`](studies/01-early-exploration/llamacpp-lookup-comparison/run/RESULTS.md) showed that `llama.cpp` answered `20/20` table-lookup prompts concisely where an earlier quantized QGRE pipeline failed on `15/20`, preventing a quantization/engine discrepancy from being misdiagnosed as a circuit failure.
- **Falsifying static single-channel attribution:** In [`WEIGHT-SPACE-ORIENTATION.md`](studies/01-early-exploration/WEIGHT-SPACE-ORIENTATION.md) (`mechanism-study`), inspecting the failing lookup `Lom = blue -> 2` showed that five of eight query heads in layer 29 already ranked the prompt's `blue` token first, while the largest opposing final-MLP weight-activation products (e.g., channel `4302` at $-5.008$ and channel `10884` at $+5.379$) fired even more strongly on *correct* answers (`3.5` on `red`, `-2.75` on valid `blue`).

**Pivot to Residual Trace Theory:** Consolidated on November 6 in [`hypothesis/RESIDUAL-TRACE-THEORY.md`](hypothesis/RESIDUAL-TRACE-THEORY.md), these negative checks ruled out searching for isolated "bad weights" or static activation templates. Capabilities are enacted *horizontally* through connected, context-dependent transformations across layers; recognizing recurring partial residue in live execution traces became the central research program.

## Chapter 2: Native BF16 Instrumentation & Physics Trace Baselines (Nov 6–7)

To study multi-step reasoning where intermediate steps are explicit and auditable without relying on packed 4-bit quantization (`Q4_K`), we established a conservation-of-energy physics suite and built custom C++ instrumentation inside our pinned `llama.cpp` engine ([`code/signal-trace/recorder/`](code/signal-trace/recorder/), [`studies/02-physics-trace-observation/`](studies/02-physics-trace-observation/)):

- **Why native BF16 mattered:** On the 24-question physics baseline ([`FINDINGS.md`](publications/findings/FINDINGS.md)), moving from mixed `Q4_K` to native `BF16` (`318` BF16 and `283` FP32 tensors) improved explicit verdicts from `8/24` to `13/24` and supplied-arithmetic accuracy from `1/6` to `4/6`, while complete multi-step physical chains remained `2/18` (`0/18` wholly defect-free prose)—exposing genuine, systematic reasoning bugs in full precision.
- **Bitwise-qualified 105-merge C++ recorder:** We built `observe` and the reduced-retention [`observe_stream.cpp`](code/signal-trace/recorder/observe_stream.cpp) ([`STREAM-RECORDER.md`](code/signal-trace/recorder/STREAM-RECORDER.md)) to record all three residual merges per block (`attn_out`, `pe_in` / FFN merge, and `pe_out` / per-layer-input merge $\implies \mathbf{105}\text{ merges per token}$) via scheduler callbacks with zero per-op `cudaDeviceSynchronize`. Qualification reproduced ordinary inference bit-for-bit across **13,462** Q4 decisions and **12,023** BF16 decisions (matching every selected token and full vocabulary logit hash).
- **Recurring trace structure vs. transient activation patching:** Across 12 paired physics comparisons, relative trajectory separation contracted across FF14 and expanded across FF15 on **846/846** Q4 decisions and **1,707/1,708** BF16 decisions ([`PHYSICS-TRACE-BF16.md`](apps/decode-machine/PHYSICS-TRACE-BF16.md)). However, replacing a single prediction's activation contribution (`physics-intervention`, `1,860` donor/recipient decisions) changed **0 emitted tokens**.

**Lesson:** Autoregressive generation rapidly absorbs a one-step activation nudge; altering a capability requires a persistent change in the **stored BF16 weights** that acts coherently across successive generated positions.

## Chapter 3: Single-Pair Rank-1 Weight Edits & Background Conditioning (Nov 7 · Paper 1)

Round 1 ([`studies/03-round-1-single-pair-edits/`](studies/03-round-1-single-pair-edits/), reported in Paper 1: [`reports/fixing-a-reasoning-mistake-by-editing-the-weights.pdf`](reports/fixing-a-reasoning-mistake-by-editing-the-weights.pdf)) tested a tightly controlled progression (`A` $\to$ `B` $\to$ `C` $\to$ `D1`) editing only `blk.16.attn_output.weight` ($1536 \times 2048 = 3{,}145{,}728$ BF16 entries) from a single paired prompt contrast (`3 kg` block climbing `1 m` [correct: `60 J / 30 J / YES`] vs. `3 m` [wrong: `60 J / 120 J / NO`, double-counting descent height]):

1. **Candidate `A` ([`A-weight-edit/STUDY.md`](studies/03-round-1-single-pair-edits/A-weight-edit/STUDY.md)):** Rank-1 update $\Delta W = (y_{\text{succ}} - y_{\text{fail}})x_{\text{fail}}^\top / \|x_{\text{fail}}\|^2$ rounded to native BF16 ($200{,}965$ entries changed, $0.02966\%$ matrix norm, `0` bytes changed outside `blk.16.attn_output`). On free generation, `A` repaired the `3 m` block to **`60 / 90 / NO`**—computing the recipient's own $3\text{ m} \times 30\text{ N} = 90\text{ J}$ rather than copying the donor's $30\text{ J}$—also repaired a sled prompt (`0/40/NO` $\to$ **`80/40/YES`**), and preserved **7/7** prior correct chains (`7/28 -> 9/28` complete chains). However, it broke a `4 kg, 2 m` block (`60/80/NO` $\to$ `60/40/YES`).
2. **Candidate `B` ([`B-selective-footprint`](studies/03-round-1-single-pair-edits/B-selective-footprint/run/REPORT.md)):** Suppressing action on 9 protected inputs while anchoring only prediction 235 shrank downstream action at the token-divergence step 269 ($0.7212\% \to 0.06969\%$) and **lost the repair** (`60/120/NO` returned), proving that single-position anchoring fails to sustain a multi-step computation.
3. **Candidate `C` ([`C-connected-preservation`](studies/03-round-1-single-pair-edits/C-connected-preservation/run/REPORT.md)):** Preserving `A`'s action across **304 connected positive inputs** while suppressing the 9 protected inputs passed the declared conjunction: it kept both repairs (`90 J` and `80 J`), restored the protected `4 kg` block (`60/80/NO`), and preserved all **7/7** original correct chains.
4. **Candidate `D1` ([`D-background-sensitivity`](studies/03-round-1-single-pair-edits/D-background-sensitivity/run/REPORT.md)):** Auditing `A` and `C` on unrelated text/math prompts showed the calibration mean vector drove **93.16%** (`A`) and **91.91%** (`C`) of squared update action. Conditioning the rank-1 read with an inverse-second-moment background covariance (`D1`, $331{,}670$ BF16 values changed, $0.04522\%$ matrix norm) retained every numerical repair and preservation property of `C` while reducing RMS background action by **85.50%** on generic requests and **69.65%** on language controls.

**Boundary & Pivot:** Despite `D1`'s surgical selectivity, performance on four unseen physics prompts stayed **1/4 $\to$ 1/4** (and an early ad-hoc multi-case candidate `E` was audited and withdrawn in [`2026-11-07-e-withdrawal.md`](journal/2026-11-07-e-withdrawal.md)). A single prompt pair inevitably entangles problem-specific quantities with the capability defect; isolating the capability itself required overlapping many varied executions.

## Chapter 4: Multi-Trace Overlaps & First Generalizing Weight Repairs (Nov 7–10 · Papers 2 & 3)

Moving from one pair to many traces produced the project's **first genuine out-of-sample capability transfers**—and triggered its sharpest methodological tightening.

### 4.1 Contextual Multi-Case Edits (`FK` and `old A2`, Paper 2)
Before the Round-2 governance reset, two multi-matrix edits were fitted across 45 construction questions and tested on **40 excluded questions** featuring unseen initial speeds ($5, 7\text{ m/s}$ vs. $4, 6, 8\text{ m/s}$ in construction) and unseen final climb heights ($2, 4\text{ m}$ vs. $1, 3\text{ m}$), documented in Paper 2 ([`reports/how-the-trace-derived-edit-works.pdf`](reports/how-the-trace-derived-edit-works.pdf)) and reconstructed from per-question records in [`records/retrospectives/2026-11-09-round-two-repairs/STUDY.md`](records/retrospectives/2026-11-09-round-two-repairs/STUDY.md):
- **`FK` (3 matrices: `blk.11.ffn_down` + `blk.14.attn_k/v`):** Repaired **15/20** wrong or missing required-climb fields on the 40 unseen questions (writing explicit $mgH$ products with the new masses and heights), though collateral descent-sign damage from `F` left complete unseen answers at **2/31** repaired and **8/9** prior correct damaged.
- **`old A2` (46 `attn_output` + `ffn_down` matrices across 23 layers):** Repaired **25/36** broader capability errors (**14/18** targeted climb errors) on the 67-question development set, and repaired **9/31** complete unseen wrong answers plus **15/20** unseen climb fields on the 40 excluded questions (raising total complete triples from **9/40 $\to$ 12/40**, while damaging **6/9** prior correct complete answers). Ablating only the 27 natural-success zero-change rows collapsed development repairs from **25/36 $\to$ 0/36** and damaged **31/31** correct answers—proving preservation rows actively shaped the repair direction.

**Why Marcos Restarted Round 2 ([`2026-11-07-clarifications.md`](journal/2026-11-07-clarifications.md), [`2026-11-07-multitrace-purpose.md`](journal/2026-11-07-multitrace-purpose.md), [`2026-11-07-round-two-restart.md`](journal/2026-11-07-round-two-restart.md)):** Although `FK` and `old A2` proved weight edits could generalize arithmetic procedures to unseen numbers, their targets came from hand-paired contextual mates and arbitrary thresholds (`1%` gap share, `7.2%` matrix cap) rather than an independent multi-trace overlap. Marcos withdrew that first attempt and established [`round-2-method.json`](studies/04-round-2-overlap/round-2-method.json) + [`verify_round2.py`](code/signal-trace/pipeline/research_guard/verify_round2.py): edit locations had to emerge from full-trace, 105-merge computational overlap with zero magic numbers.

### 4.2 Pure 60-Trace DTW Computational Overlap (`a2-path`, Paper 3)
Under the strict Round-2 rules, we captured 30 completely correct traces and 30 wrong traces sharing the same first error (subtracting downhill gravitational work $KE_0 - mgd$) on GPU0 (`30,546` total generated token steps) and aligned every trace across all 105 merges by DTW on unit-normalized turn vectors alone ([`a2-path/declaration.json`](studies/04-round-2-overlap/runs/a2-path/declaration.json), [`a2-path/result.json`](studies/04-round-2-overlap/runs/a2-path/result.json), Paper 3: [`reports/how-the-path-edit-works.pdf`](reports/how-the-path-edit-works.pdf)):
- **Emergent localization:** Without any token or position hint, the 60 traces separated in one contiguous computational stretch—aligned places `142–313`—at **5.8$\times$** sampling variation with split-half cosine agreement **0.825**.
- **Path-ordered weight edit & transfer:** Solving sequentially in execution order across the first **18 matrices** (layers `0–8`, stopping prospectively when layer 9's teacher-forced row drift reached the size of the requested move) flipped **29/30** fitted wrong answers from subtracting to adding gravitational work (**17/30** fully complete; **18/30** originally correct kept complete; overall `55/60` adding descent vs. `30/60` before). On Round 1's unseen 28-question suite, it completed **7/21** previously wrong chains (**11/21** adding descent, **3/7** prior correct kept complete).

## Chapter 5: Productive Falsifications — Why Variance Tubes, State Targets & Token Losses Failed (Nov 10–15)

`a2-path` repaired the targeted sign in `29/30` cases yet introduced new arithmetic regressions (such as misreporting post-climb energy as available energy or halving/doubling initial kinetic energy) and reworded `49/60` openings. Between November 10 and November 15, the lab systematically tested—and falsified—four candidate remedies (cataloged across [`studies/04-round-2-overlap/RUN-CATALOG.md`](studies/04-round-2-overlap/RUN-CATALOG.md)):

1. **Post-hoc protection rungs (`b2-path`, `c2-path`, `d2-path`) & FP32 detours ([`2026-11-10-controlled-training.md`](journal/2026-11-10-controlled-training.md)):** Retrofitting protection onto a finished multi-matrix edit degraded performance (`3/3` refit/rescale attempts did worse). Converting editable matrices to FP32 hid quantization floor behavior and was rejected: training had to hold overlapping regions steady *during* optimization in native BF16.
2. **Strict per-layer variance tubes (`controlled-bf16-1..4`, [`2026-11-12-correction-not-protection.md`](journal/2026-11-12-correction-not-protection.md)):** Enforcing hard per-matrix row/mean variance bounds (`tube.py`) treated every intermediate layer's state spread as sacred. Either BF16 rounding ate the constrained step, or downstream blocks were barred from compensating small upstream perturbations. Marcos replaced separate "protection" constraints with a single **computational correction objective**: zero direct gradient where computation already agrees within natural witness variation, positive gradient only where it diverges.
3. **Teacher-forced single sweeps & lexical/token judges ([`2026-11-14-iterative-gap-closure.md`](journal/2026-11-14-iterative-gap-closure.md)):** Route audits (`sign-route-a2-path`) showed part of `a2-path`'s own-answer `+` rode on shifted opening wording, while teacher-forced inputs drifted away from the edited model's true free-generation trajectory. All word, token-logit, and parsed-answer progress instruments were structurally excised from the trainer: the model had to regenerate freely after each update and be judged strictly on its 105-merge trace.
4. **Post-merge state-value targets (`success-sets-loop`, [`2026-11-15-computation-not-state-values.md`](journal/2026-11-15-computation-not-state-values.md)):** Pulling post-merge residual vectors $h^{\text{out}}$ toward convex hulls of successful answers inadvertently pulled donor problem content into the recipient. `success-sets-loop` was halted cleanly at matrix 42 of pass 1, permanently establishing the relational **branch turn** $T_{j,m} = \hat{y}_{j,m} - \hat{x}_{j,m}$ calibrated against $K = 14$ non-self witness traces (`vector-chance-original`) as the training observable.

## Chapter 6: Iterative Computational Backprop, Causal Transplants & Operation Signals (Nov 16–22 · Paper 4)

### 6.1 Direct Computational Backpropagation (`0 -> 390`, Paper 4)
Initially, step 5 ran an inner conjugate-gradient least-squares (`CGLS`) linear inverse per step. On November 16 ([`2026-11-16-direct-computational-update.md`](journal/2026-11-16-direct-computational-update.md), [`reports/Iterative_Learning_from_Computational_Traces.docx`](reports/Iterative_Learning_from_Computational_Traces.docx), [`FINAL-EXPERIMENT.md`](FINAL-EXPERIMENT.md)), we replaced inner linear inversion with **iterative response-scaled weight-gradient descent** across all **538 native parameter tensors**:
- Compute VJP gradient $S = -\nabla_W L(W)$ and JVP response $q = J_r S$ through the 35-block PyTorch graph (`full_graph.py`), scale by $\alpha = -\langle r, q\rangle / \langle q, q\rangle$, accumulate exact FP32 rounding remainders (`full_state.py`), regenerate freely in `llama.cpp`, and realign via DTW.
- When diagnosing finite-forecast vs. native `llama.cpp` discrepancies, factorial Q/K/V substitutions traced a sign flip to FP16 KV-cache quantization in early attention producers; anchoring original native keys across all 15 KV producers (`native_key_anchor.py`, [`2026-11-18-installed-native-key-anchor`](records/development/2026-11-18-installed-native-key-anchor/README.md)) restored exact sign agreement and cut finite projection error by **90.66%**.
- **Trajectory & Unseen Panel (`0 -> 390`, [`reports/descent-trace-repair/paper.pdf`](reports/descent-trace-repair/paper.pdf), [`training-and-stopping.md`](final-experiment/training-and-stopping.md)):** Fresh computational loss on `discovery-05-channel-h1` fell **78.09%** ($0.00029234 \to 0.00006406$ at checkpoint `390`), repairing the generated physics chain from `64 - 40 - 13 = 11 J (NO)` to **`64 + 40 - 13 = 91 J (YES)`** while preserving rough-patch friction (`-13 J`) and keeping the `44 J` reference chain intact (`expanded-004-h1`). Even when discrete route switches caused one-step relapses at updates `356`, `371`, and `387`, the unchanged trace loop recovered `91 J` on the very next update (`357`, `372`, `388`). Checkpoints `175`, `189`, and `348` also repaired the climb requirement on the unfitted `115 J` problem (`60 -> 30 J`). On a blind-graded 8-formulation unseen energy panel ([`paired-outcomes.json`](studies/04-round-2-overlap/runs/frozen-energy-generalization/paired-outcomes.json)), complete valid chains rose from **1/8 (stock) $\to$ 2/8 (`175/189`) $\to$ 3/8 (`390`)**, while unseen downhill signs alone were `4/8 -> 3/8` (2 errors + 1 cap fixed, 1 prior success regressed).

### 6.2 Causal Weight-Family Transplants & Operation-Specific Trace Signals
To understand *how* the learner repaired `91 J` and why single-reference training did not yet fix every unseen descent, we ran targeted causal interventions and operation-level overlap studies:
- **Redundant causal sufficiency across weight families ([`2026-11-17-descent-sign-swap/README.md`](records/development/2026-11-17-descent-sign-swap/README.md)):** Transplanting *only* the 35 `attn_output` matrices ($785{,}007$ BF16 diffs) or *only* the 35 `ffn_down` matrices ($1{,}368{,}221$ BF16 diffs) from a repaired descendant onto the failing `early-27` model (`11 J`) **each independently restored `64 + 40 = 104 J` and `91 J / YES`**, recovering **99.84%** and **99.21%** of the full trace-loss reduction. Across all 73 shared-prefix predictions before token divergence, the two independent repairs shifted the 105-merge turn field along a shared direction (cosine **0.7364**, **0.9991** at block-20 FFN) that a failed edit lacked (**0.2991 / 0.2006**, [`same-input-operation-comparison`](studies/04-round-2-overlap/runs/same-input-operation-comparison/README.md)).
- **Clean 105-merge Climb Signal ([`joint-climb-conditional-overlap`](studies/04-round-2-overlap/runs/joint-climb-conditional-overlap/README.md)):** Conditioning across 16 downhill-subtracting traces isolated a 712-cell 105-merge climb contrast that classified the withheld `115 J` extra-height error on **643/673** cells and dropped **86.9%** ($0.06863 \to 0.00899$) when checkpoint `175` repaired its climb ($60 \to 30\text{ J}$).
- **Pre-Choice Descent vs. Friction Localization ([`operand-identity.md`](final-experiment/operand-identity.md), [`unbanded-control-readback`](records/development/2026-11-18-unbanded-control-readback/README.md), [`pure-descent-native-read`](records/retrospectives/2026-11-18-pure-descent-native-read/README.md)):** Every stock answer that subtracted gravitational work *also* subtracted friction correctly. Our phase-matched turn detector flagged **15/15** (local) and **13/13** (landmark) wrong descents immediately before operator selection with **0/30** false alarms on valid friction, caught the unseen reordered error (`discovery-02-reordered-h3`), dropped **99.981% / 99.990%** on the two causal transplants, and in a 1-step isolated pullback on `early-27` moved the native `+` vs. `-` logit margin on fixed history from **$-2.5242 \to -0.0465$**. (Meanwhile, adding an unweighted auxiliary prefix term onto already-repaired `390 -> 401` yielded `0/8` new unseen repairs, honestly reported in Paper 4.)

### 6.3 Diagnosing the Unanchored Joint Loop & Defining the Unified Frontier (Nov 18–22)
On November 18–19, `joint-stock-prechoice-training` attempted to repair *both* stock failures simultaneously (`91 J` descent + `95 J` climb, with `44 J` preserved) from unedited stock weights ([`fresh-fit-readback.json`](studies/04-round-2-overlap/runs/joint-stock-prechoice-training/fresh-fit-readback.json), [`2026-11-21-consolidation-takeover.md`](journal/2026-11-21-consolidation-takeover.md)):
- **What happened:** Update 1 repaired the `95 J` required climb ($120 \to \mathbf{40\text{ J}}$) and cut pre-choice squared error by **28.03%**, yet logged group loss fell ($0.0272 \to 0.0174$ by revision 3) while wrong descents spread from **1/3 $\to$ 3/3** answers!
- **Root-cause diagnosis ([`2026-11-22-joint-loss-recheck/README.md`](records/development/2026-11-22-joint-loss-recheck/README.md)):**
  1. **Missing whole-trace anchor in the gradient:** `group_contrast_driver.py` differentiated *only* the sparse labelled-operation cells while merely logging the whole-trace reference loss—which surged **13$\times$** ($0.000292 \to 0.00387$). Without the whole-trace reference acting as a computational "KL trust region," shared weights drifted off-manifold, inflating preserved-operation error **51$\times$** ($0.00034 \to 0.01753$).
  2. **Matcher dropout:** When off-manifold drift caused the stage matcher to abstain on a degraded operation, `group_contrast_objective.py` added `0` to the logged loss. Re-scoring the exact MLflow step artifacts with every lost cell charged its last measured value proved that Update 1 was a genuine **-28.6%** improvement, whereas subsequent updates drove the true carried loss to **+22.6%** *above* stock (and **+42.0%** on the `3,845` common cells).

Following the November 21–22 archive verification—which restored all digest-verified captures and blind panel records, consolidated the 137-file paper bundle ([`publications/descent-paper-release/`](publications/descent-paper-release/)), and bound 63 unarchived working files into `recorded_losses.py` with 124 guard tests passing—the next experiment is sharply defined: **return the differentiated whole-trace reference anchor to the gradient alongside calibrated operation-specific terms, and score/stop on carried loss where unaligned stages retain their last measured penalty.**
